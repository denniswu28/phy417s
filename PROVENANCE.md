## Measured data

**This repository retains no measured data** (`GS-SYN-001`).

Dennis states that he and Alex Tong collected the historical measurements
together and that permission covers their public posting and redistribution
(`GS-COLLAB-001`, `GS-DATA-001`). That statement is recorded as his; the
**grantor and the documentary permission source were not supplied** and remain
`NEEDS_REVIEW`. No legal conclusion is drawn.

The measured files are therefore withheld regardless of the permission
statement. Reintroducing any of them — in whole or as a subset — requires a
separate evidence review and separate owner approval at its own gate, with
recorded checksums and per-file provenance. It is not authorised by this work
and must never happen silently inside an unrelated change.

Every spectrum in this repository is generated by
[`src/galaxy_structure/synthetic.py`](src/galaxy_structure/synthetic.py) from a
committed configuration and an explicit seed, and is labelled `synthetic` in the
filename, the file header, every figure caption, the manifest, and the README.
